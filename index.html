Welcome to My Training & Recovery Hub

This website breaks down my personal fitness routine based on what has worked best for me. Do not treat this website as a be-all and end-all, but more of a general guide based on what's worked for me over my 2+ years of training. Rather than overcomplicating my workouts with trendy exercises, I organize my training into a classic Push, Pull, Legs (PPL) split paired with simple daily nutrition and a four-item supplement stack. All exercises are performed with a warm-up set followed by two hard sets to failure.

A PPL split groups muscles that naturally work together into the same session. This keeps workouts efficient and gives each muscle group plenty of recovery time before it is trained again. Ensure you are taking rest days aswell, as your body needs time to recover. I usually add a rest day or two in between my leg days and push days.
