Daily Fuel

Nutrition, Recovery & Supplements

Training breaks muscle down, while adequate rest, nutrition, and sleep build it back up. Focusing on daily macro targets, fiber, hydration, and consistent sleep covers what matters most, while a simple supplement routine fills in the gaps.

Six Core Nutrition & Recovery Priorities

  • Adequate Daily Protein: Protein is essential for repairing and building muscle tissue after lifting. Aiming for roughly 0.8 to 1 gram of protein per pound of body weight spread across meals works well.
  • Sufficient Carbohydrates: Carbs are your body's primary fuel source for intense workouts. Eating enough carbs keeps gym performance high and replenishes muscle glycogen.
  • Moderate Dietary Fat: Healthy fats are needed for hormone production and vitamin absorption, but keeping fat intake moderate prevents calories from getting too high and leaves room for protein and carbs.
  • Enough Daily Fiber: Tracking fiber is just as important as tracking macros. Getting around 25 to 38 grams of fiber per day supports digestion, gut health, and fullness.
  • Consistent Hydration: Drinking plenty of water throughout the day keeps strength levels up, helps nutrient transport, and prevents cramping during workouts.
  • Quality Sleep: Aim for 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night. Sleep is when growth hormone release peaks and your central nervous system recovers from heavy training.

My Daily Supplement Stack

Supplements do not replace hard training or a solid diet. I stick to four basics that support strength, general health, and sleep quality:

5g Daily

Creatine Monohydrate

Supports strength and muscle recovery by helping replenish ATP during heavy sets and drawing water into muscle cells.

With a Fatty Meal

Omega-3 Fish Oil (EPA + DHA)

Supports heart, brain, and joint health. Look at the combined EPA and DHA content on the label rather than just total fish oil milligrams.

Morning / With Food

General Multivitamin

Acts as nutritional insurance to cover any small micronutrient gaps in daily food intake.

Before Bed

Magnesium Glycinate

Taken at night to support muscle relaxation, nervous system recovery, and deeper sleep while being easy on the stomach. Ensure you get magnesium glycinate, and not other forms like magnesium oxide as humans absorb this type better.
